# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free project.

Be considerate, discuss technical decisions in good faith, give actionable
feedback, and respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not
accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through
the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Reports will be handled with
care and shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
